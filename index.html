Three steps to a live call

  1. 1

    Allow your microphone

    Use Chrome, Edge or Safari on a laptop or phone. Headphones keep the audio clean.

  2. 2

    Press Start call

    Pick one assistant and speak naturally. You can interrupt, just like on a real call.

  3. 3

    Follow the script, or don’t

    Each assistant has a sample call to follow. Go off-script to see how it handles the unexpected.

The assistants

Choose an assistant, then press Start call.

One call at a time. The other assistant waits until your call ends.

Good to know

Calls run in your browser

Allow microphone access when asked. If you blocked it, use the icon in the address bar to allow it again.

Hand-overs are simulated

When an assistant transfers you to a specialist or a human agent, it says so and the call ends.

Nothing is sent to your phone

The recharge link, journey link and feedback survey are not delivered in this demo, and phone numbers aren’t checked on browser calls.

Demo data only

Card details come live from a demo database, not real Nol accounts. Answers come from each assistant’s knowledge base.
